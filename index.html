The diagnostic

The IVA Decision Readiness Score.

A decision is ready when the organization can explain what is being decided, why now, who owns it, what evidence supports it, what resources it requires, what obligations shape it, and how implementation will be tracked.

0-5

Evidence clarity

The decision has usable evidence, not just more material.

0-5

Authority clarity

The right person or body can actually decide.

0-5

Capacity realism

The labor, timing, and operating burden are visible.

0-5

Funding alignment

The financial assumptions support the action requested.

0-5

Accountability clarity

Owners, obligations, records, and follow-through are explicit.
