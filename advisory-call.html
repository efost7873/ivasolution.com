Decision Readiness Call

One focused hour around one stuck decision.

The Decision Readiness Call is a virtual working session for one defined decision, board packet, reporting burden, funding question, capacity constraint, program issue, or implementation gap.

IVA applies the five-part readiness frame: evidence clarity, authority clarity, capacity realism, funding alignment, and accountability clarity.

$200/hour, 1-hour minimum. Payment required before scheduling. After payment, you will be redirected to the booking page.

Best used when:

You already have a real decision pressure in view and need the structure around it made clearer before the next meeting, escalation, proposal, investment, vote, or implementation step.

What the call focuses on.

The call starts with one issue and tests whether the decision has enough structure to move. The purpose is not to create another report. It is to find the missing readiness condition.

Decision path

Where authority, accountability, evidence, timing, and consequence no longer sit together clearly enough to support action.

Capacity pressure

Where work gets completed only because people absorb hidden load, repeat steps, rebuild context, or carry responsibility without enough control.

Funding and obligation fit

Where budgets, funder rules, public accountability, compliance expectations, stakeholder impact, or reporting duties need to be represented before action.

How payment and scheduling work.

Virtual calls are prepaid through Stripe. After payment, Stripe should send you to the IVA booking page so you can choose a time.

In-person, group, executive, and board sessions are scheduled by invoice because travel, group size, preparation, and session length may affect scope.

Bring enough context to work on the real issue.

The call works best when the decision is specific. You do not need a polished packet. Bring the materials already being used to make, explain, report, approve, fund, or defend the decision.

The decision

One board item, approval question, funding path, reporting obligation, program choice, implementation gap, or operational bottleneck.

The evidence

Existing reports, notes, dashboards, board materials, grant requirements, program documents, rate studies, or operating examples.

The pressure

What is stuck, delayed, duplicated, hidden, underfunded, overburdened, or at risk if the structure stays the same.

What you leave with.

The call is designed to produce a usable next step, not a broad opinion. The focus stays on structure, readiness, and decision usefulness.

A sharper decision frame

The issue is translated from general pressure into a visible pattern across decision, owner, timing, evidence, and implementation condition.

An initial readiness read

You can see which readiness dimensions are strong, which are weak, and which form of evidence or authority needs better standing.

A practical next step

The next move may be narrowing the issue, preparing a triage, scoping a sprint, changing a decision path, or improving an evidence practice.

Start with one real decision.

Useful examples include a stuck board item, a grant reporting burden, a rate-study implementation gap, a workflow that keeps getting rebuilt, a funding question, a capacity constraint, or a decision the organization protects but cannot clearly explain.
