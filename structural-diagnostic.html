Decision Readiness Sprint

Map the structure behind a decision that has to move.

The Decision Readiness Sprint examines one significant decision across evidence clarity, authority clarity, capacity realism, funding alignment, and accountability clarity.

Request Sprint Proposal Back to services

$5,000 to $12,000 for standard sprints. Complex public, infrastructure, or multi-stakeholder work is quoted by scope.

Best used when:

A board, funder, executive, public body, or implementation team has enough material to see the issue, but not enough structure to make the decision hold.

What the sprint examines.

IVA reviews existing documents, decision paths, evidence flows, operating routines, funding assumptions, reporting obligations, and leadership context.

Evidence clarity

Which evidence can actually support the decision, which evidence is missing, and which material creates noise without improving readiness.

Authority clarity

Where decision rights, recommendations, approvals, implementation owners, and accountability lines are unclear or split apart.

Capacity realism

Where hidden labor, rework, escalation, correction loops, or leadership bottlenecks are preserving delivery while weakening the system.

Funding alignment

How budget, debt, grants, customer burden, staffing, timeline, and resource assumptions support or undermine the decision.

Accountability and obligations

Which public, funder, equity, stakeholder, compliance, reporting, or operational obligations need to shape the decision.

Implementation path

What owners, records, cadence, routines, and next actions would be needed to move from approval to implementation.

What the work produces.

The sprint produces a Decision Readiness Score, evidence map, decision memo, readiness gaps, implementation path, and accountability structure.

Complex, public-sector, statewide, infrastructure, multi-function, board-facing, or multi-stakeholder scopes are quoted by proposal.
